Vol.1 Roast Lab — Mariam Tower

The flagship space: marble counters, daily roasting on view, and a queue that starts before opening.

There's a specific kind of quiet that settles over Vol.1's Mariam Tower location around 8am — the espresso machine warming up, the roaster ticking through its first batch of the day, regulars filing in before the Sharq business crowd arrives. This is the flagship, and it earns the title.

The space

Marble accents, glass-walled roasting visible from the counter, and a layout that manages to feel both minimal and warm — a balance a lot of Kuwait's newer specialty cafés reach for and few actually land. Seating is limited enough that this isn't a laptop café, which feels intentional: this is a coffee-first room.

"The cereal cold brew shouldn't work on paper. It works completely in the cup."

— tasting note, first visit

The coffee

The single-origin program rotates, but the Kenyan V60 on our visit was the clear standout — bright, structured acidity with a long finish that held up even as the cup cooled. The house espresso is tuned for the region's preference for slightly sweeter shots, and it's a smart call: approachable without dulling the underlying bean quality.

The much-discussed cereal cold brew is a genuine signature drink rather than a gimmick — worth ordering even if it sounds like a novelty on the menu.

The verdict

4.6
out of 5
Worth the trip, worth the queue. A flagship that actually earns the word — come for the Kenyan V60, stay for the roasting theatre. Best for a slow morning rather than a quick grab-and-go.
